## Project Overview
The objective of this project is to use generative design to develop a lightweight skateboard truck hanger with an integrated motor mount. The design aims to minimize mass while maintaining structural integrity, with a safety factor of 2, and consolidating separate components into a single structure.

## Load Case Definition

| Name | Type | Magnitude | Location | Rationale |
|------|------|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Rider Weight** | Bearing Load | 1000 N (~225 lb) | Axle cylindrical faces<br><img src="/assets/img/rider_weight.png" alt="axle-load" style="width:300px;"/> | Represents rider weight under dynamic conditions. A value of 1000 N approximates a ~100 kg rider with additional margin for motion-induced amplification. Applied as a bearing load to simulate realistic force transfer through wheel bearings. |
| **Rider Weight (Pivot Cup)** | Bearing Load (Normal Force) | 800 N | Pivot cup interface<br><img src="/assets/img/pivot_weight.png" alt="pivot-load" style="width:300px;"/> | Represents normal force transmitted through the pivot cup due to rider weight and leaning during turning, capturing load transfer through the steering mechanism. |
| **Motor Torque** | Remote Moment | 6 N·m | Motor shaft center<br><img src="/assets/img/motor_torque.png" alt="motor-torque" style="width:300px;"/> | Represents torque generated by the Flipsky motor (6374 ). This drives the belt system and introduces torsional loading into the motor mount and hanger. |
| **Motor Weight** | Remote Force | 35 N (~8 lb) | Preserved regions around motor mounting slots<br><img src="/assets/img/motor_weight.png" alt="motor-weight" style="width:300px;"/> | Models gravitational load of the motor. A conservative value was used for robustness. |
| **Belt Tension (Drive Force)** | Remote Force | 400 N | Tangential to motor pulley / shaft<br><img src="/assets/img/belt_force.png" alt="belt-force" style="width:300px;"/> | Represents belt tension during motor actuation. |
| **Belt Drive Torque (Axle Input)** | Remote Moment | 300 N·mm (0.3 N·m) | Axle/bearing interface<br><img src="/assets/img/belt_torque.png" alt="belt-torque" style="width:300px;"/> | Torsional load applied at the axle to represent belt-driven actuation. |
| **Turning (Shear) – Case 1** | Force | 400 N | Lateral to axle (normal to cylindrical face)<br><img src="/assets/img/turning1.png" alt="turning-load-1" style="width:300px;"/> | Simulates lateral forces during carving. Represents shear due to rider lean. |
| **Turning (Shear) – Case 2** | Force | 400 N (opposite direction) | Same as above, reversed direction<br><img src="/assets/img/turning2.png" alt="turning-load-2" style="width:300px;"/> | Ensures performance under both left and right turning scenarios by reversing shear direction. |
| **Bump / Impact Load** | Force | 800 N | Normal to axle cylindrical face<br><img src="/assets/img/bump.png" alt="impact-load" style="width:300px;"/> | Represents transient forces from bumps or curbs. Applied to both axles. |
