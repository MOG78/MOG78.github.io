## Load Case Definition

| Name | Type | Magnitude | Location | Rationale |
|------|------|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Rider Weight** | Bearing Load | 1000 N (~225 lb) | Axle cylindrical faces<br>![Axle Load](/assets/img/rider_weight.png) | Represents rider weight under dynamic conditions. A value of 1000 N approximates a ~100 kg rider with additional margin for motion-induced amplification. Applied as a bearing load to simulate realistic force transfer through wheel bearings. |
| **Rider Weight (Pivot Cup)** | Bearing Load (Normal Force) | 800 N | Pivot cup interface<br>![Pivot Load](/assets/img/pivot_weight.png) | Represents normal force transmitted through the pivot cup due to rider weight and leaning during turning, capturing load transfer through the steering mechanism. |
| **Motor Torque** | Remote Moment | 6 N·m | Motor shaft center<br>![Motor Torque](/assets/img/motor_torque.png) | Represents torque generated by the Flipsky motor (6374 ). This drives the belt system and introduces torsional loading into the motor mount and hanger. |
| **Motor Weight** | Remote Force | 35 N (~8 lb) | Preserved regions around motor mounting slots<br>![Motor Weight](/assets/img/motor_weight.png) | Models gravitational load of the motor. A conservative value was used to account for vibration and inertial effects, ensuring structural robustness. |
| **Belt Tension (Drive Force)** | Remote Force | 400 N | Tangential to motor pulley / shaft<br>![Belt Force](/assets/img/belt_force.png) | Represents belt tension during torque transmission. This introduces bending and shear into the motor mount and reflects high-load conditions such as acceleration or hill climbing. |
| **Belt Drive Torque (Axle Input)** | Remote Moment | 300 N·mm (0.3 N·m) | Axle/bearing interface<br>![Belt Torque](/assets/img/belt_torque.png) | Prescribed torsional load applied at the axle to represent belt-driven actuation in the generative design model. This value was used as a conservative design input to capture torsional loading effects during motor operation, including acceleration and incline conditions. |
| **Turning (Shear) – Case 1** | Force | 400 N | Lateral to axle (normal to cylindrical face)<br>![Turning Load](/assets/img/turning1.png) | Simulates lateral forces during carving. Represents shear due to rider lean and inertial effects. |
| **Turning (Shear) – Case 2** | Force | 400 N (opposite direction) | Same as above, reversed direction<br>![Turning Load Reverse](/assets/img/turning2.png) | Ensures performance under both left and right turning scenarios by reversing shear direction. |
| **Bump / Impact Load** | Force | 800 N | Normal to axle cylindrical face<br>![Impact Load](/assets/img/bump.png) | Represents transient forces from bumps or curbs. Value exceeds static rider weight to capture dynamic amplification. |
