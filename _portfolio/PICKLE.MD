#### Pickleball Paddle Design and Game Viability
The paddle face and overall geometry were created to resemble the size and proportions of standard paddles to maintain usability during play, but at a relatively smaller size to save space on the print bed. The final paddle was printed using PLA and successfully used to hit pickleballs during testing. The structure was able to withstand repeated impacts with the ball. However, the paddle was not tested through a full game of pickleball. 

#### TPU Handle Element
The design  included a TPU component integrated into the handle region to improve grip and comfort during gameplay. TPU was selected because it is a flexible filament that can provide increased friction and shock absorption. However, due to time constraints during fabrication, the TPU component was not included in the final printed paddle. As a result, the final handle was printed entirely in PLA, which made the grip more rigid and less comfortable than originally intended. For extended play in the future, this element would be particularly valuable.

#### Interchangeable Edge Guard
An interchangeable edge guard was incorporated into the paddle design to protect the paddle face. TPU 95A was chosen because its elasticity allows it to absorb impact forces and reduce damage to the paddle edge during play. The guard was designed to fit along the perimeter of the paddle face and also extend under the handle insert area. This design allowed the edge guard to remain secure. The TPU edge guard was successfully printed and included in the final physical paddle.

#### Weight and Mass Balance Optimization
The paddle was printed using PLA with a rectilinear infill pattern. Rectilinear infill was selected because it provides a good balance between print efficiency and keeping the internal structure relatively lightweight. However, the final paddle still felt heavier than a typical commercial pickleball paddle. Commercial paddles often use lightweight materials or hollow core centerss, which significantly reduce weight. Because this paddle was fully printed in PLA, the density of the material contributed to the increased weight. If the design were revised, the weight could be reduced by lowering the infill percentage, incorporating more internal cavities, or using a lighter material.
